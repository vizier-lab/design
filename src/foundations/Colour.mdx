# Colour

{colorTokens.length} tokens, each with a Dark and a Light value. Both themes are
shown side by side here; everywhere else, the toolbar switch decides.

Components use them through Tailwind: `bg-surface`, `text-muted`,
`border-border-strong`. Text tokens drop the `text-` prefix (`text-fg`,
`text-soft`, `text-muted`, `text-faint`). The raw variable is `var(--vz-…)`.

Colour carries meaning, so keep to it:

- **Emerald (`live`)** means live, and only that. Always pair it with a word.
- **Lapis (`rank`)** marks owner, superadmin and grants.
- **Ember (`failed`)** is failure and destructive actions; **amber (`retrying`)** is backing off and limits.
- **`text/faint`** is under 4.5:1 on the ground in both themes: timestamps, column labels and hints only.
