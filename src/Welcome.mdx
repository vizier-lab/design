# Vizier design library

This catalogue is the **source of truth** for Vizier's interface: its tokens,
type, icons, brand pieces and components. The "Celestial Court" design was the
starting point; from here on, a change to the design is a change to this
library, reviewed in this catalogue.

## Finding your way

- **Foundations**: colour, type, size and spacing, elevation and tone.
- **Icons**: every icon in the vocabulary, by its design name.
- **Brand**: the mark, wordmark, agent sigils and the LED board.
- **Components**: grouped as the app uses them (Shell, Actions & inputs,
  Feedback, Surfaces & data, Chat, …).

Use the **theme** switch in the toolbar to see each entry in Dark, Light, or
System (follows your OS).

## Reviewing

Each component's docs page starts with its **inventory id** and the WebUI file
it will replace. Every interactive component has a **States** story showing
hover, pressed and focus side by side, and the **Accessibility** panel lists
any axe findings.

New entries carry the `needs-review` tag. Filter the sidebar by that tag to
see what is left. Approving an entry means changing its tag to `approved` in
its story file, in a commit; changes are requested as issues on
[vizier-lab/design](https://github.com/vizier-lab/design/issues) that link the
story.
